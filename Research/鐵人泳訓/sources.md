## S01 [研究] Landers et al. 2008 Swim Positioning and its Influence on Triathlon Outcome
- 機構/期刊：Int J Exerc Sci 1(3):96-105；URL https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4739296
- 全文已讀: 是（PMC 全文）
- 族群：1999 ITU World Cup draft-legal 奧運距離 20 場（男10女10），男 605、女 479 人，菁英
- 相關：H4、H1
- 關鍵：「the winner exited the water in the first pack in 90% of elite male and 70% of elite female races」；游泳名次與最終名次相關 r=0.49(男)/0.39(女)，騎 0.67，跑 0.86/0.85；作者結論最終名次較由跑步決定。游泳時間 SD 男1.48%、女3.73%。

## S02 [研究] Selles-Perez, Fernandez-Saez, Cejuela 2019 Polarized and Pyramidal TID vs Half-Ironman
- 期刊：J Sports Sci Med 18:708-715；URL https://www.jssm.org/jssm-18-708.xml
- 全文已讀: 是
- 族群：13 名（原18招募）休閒男鐵人；半超 1.9 km/90 km/21.1 km；20週（7週一般+13週特定）
- 相關：P3、H3（整體鐵人強度分布，非泳段專屬）
- 關鍵：POL zone1/2/3 =「84.5%/4.2%/11.3%」，PYR「77.9%/18.8%/3.3%」；「only two seconds differentiated both groups in the race」；zone 2 時間在 POL 與游、騎成績反相關。注意：此研究強度分布是全項目合計，不是泳段單獨。

## S03 [實務文件] Heggy 2021 How Hard Should I Work the Triathlon Swim? (USMS)
- URL https://www.usms.org/fitness-and-training/articles-and-videos/articles/how-hard-should-i-work-the-triathlon-swim
- 全文已讀: 是（工具回報完整）
- 族群：一般分齡鐵人（含 masters）
- 相關：H2、H3、H6、H1
- 關鍵：「Technique > Fitness」（拖曳、抬頭定向、直線、跟游）；做可持續配速組以學出比賽距離能守的最快速度；長組內加「25 to 100 yards/meters of all-out sprinting」再回到距離配速；起泳位置依擁擠舒適度、衝刺速度、要跟誰；以衝刺換包／回應加速。

## S04 [社群討論] Slowtwitch「The myth of triathlon swimming: swimming is swimming」
- URL https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/the-myth-of-triathlon-swimming-swimming-is-swimming/767165
- 全文已讀: 是（僅第1頁：OP+19則回覆，後有分頁）
- 族群：分齡鐵人
- 相關：H1、H7、P1、P2
- 主流意見：鐵人游得不夠。「The bottom line is triathletes do not swim enough for the distances they compete.」（Yukh0，自稱教過約500名鐵人）；多數分齡游 2–4 小時/週、≤10,000 yd。DarkSpeedWorks：「Triathlon swimming is like distance pool swimming, which is quite different from sprinting.」JasoninHalifax：2 次/週維持、5 次/週才進步。
- 反對意見：lightheir「2-4hrs is typically enough for a solid finish for an AGer triathlete」；Chemist「A 1% improvement in the bike or run makes a bigger difference」（泳約占10小時鐵人10%）。
- 約5–6人支持「需要更多/更好游泳」，約5人支持「時間花別處或2–4h已夠」。

- URL https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/swim-pool-vs-race/386382
- 全文已讀: 是（OP+9則回覆）
- 相關：H10
- 無換算係數。約5則認為開放水域較慢或距離常偏長（「yeah, you will be slower」「they are always long!」），1則穿防寒衣反而較快，1則視轉身方式。原因：定向、失去蹬牆、起游過快、賽道距離不準。

## S06 [實務文件] Newsome (Swim Smooth) 2025 Learn How to Swim Pace Like a PRO
- URL https://blog.swimsmooth.com/p/get-your-pace-right
- 全文已讀: 是（文末簽名；頁面有付費提示但本文完整）
- 相關：H2、H10、P5
- CSS：「By performing two time trials (400m and 200m), you can calculate your personal CSS pace.」「Testing every 4–6 weeks」；範例組：1000m 起 CSS+8 sec/100m 倒金字塔降至 200m @CSS。
- 開放水域：「Open water adds variables that GPS data alone can't account for.」換算表僅限游泳賽：1500m=CSS；5km=CSS+2~4 sec/100m；10km=+6~10。文章明言無鐵人專屬換算（H10 無鐵人專屬）。

## S07 [實務文件] Mackinnon 2024 How much swimming does a triathlete need to do? (Triathlon Magazine Canada)
- URL https://triathlonmagazine.ca/?p=77692
- 全文已讀: 是
- 族群：分齡（含 Kona 年齡組冠軍例）、初學者、draft-legal/菁英
- 相關：P1、P2、P4、P8、H7、H4
- 「Swimming twice a week is likely enough to maintain any swim fitness you've developed.」三次/週可進步；「Getting into the water four times a week (0r [sic] more) is optimal」用於需改進、draft-legal 賽或菁英。多數學員「45 to 60 min sessions」；初學者偏好 4×「1,000 to 1,500 m」勝於 3×「1,500 to 2,000 m」；進階例：單堂很少超過 2,500 m，每週一堂 3,000–3,500 m。「Intervals are the name of the game in the pool.」「You're not going to get a lot out of a 45 min straight swim three times a week.」無強度區數字，無按賽距計畫。

## S08 [研究] Ulsamer et al. 2014 Swimming performances in long distance open-water events with and without wetsuit
- BMC Sports Sci Med Rehabil 6:20；URL https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4041346
- 全文已讀: 是
- 族群：Lake Zurich 26.4 km（300人）、LOST-Race 3.8 km（284人）賽績資料，非鐵人賽
- 相關：P7（僅旁證，防寒衣影響）；與泳訓參數關聯弱
- 「open-water swimmers competing while wearing a wetsuit were not generally faster in both the long and the ultra-long-distance swimming event」，結論又稱防寒衣對速度有正面影響、女性在超長距離獲益較多（作者自述結果不完全一致）。
